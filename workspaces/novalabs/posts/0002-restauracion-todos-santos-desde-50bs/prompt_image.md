On a rustic dark carved cedar table, handcrafted beeswax candles gently flicker with warm amber flames (#FFA726), surrounded by traditional Bolivian baked sweet bread figures (tanta wawa and bread ladders), vibrant orange cempasúchil marigold petals, and rustic ceramic cups.
