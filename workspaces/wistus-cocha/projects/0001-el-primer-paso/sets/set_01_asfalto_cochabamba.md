## 🎬 Prompt de Generación Maestro (Background Plate / Style Reference)

Para generar el escenario vacío perfecto que servirá de fondo (Background Plate) en todas las tomas de este teaser publicitario, utiliza el siguiente prompt hiper-realista en **Google Flow / Nano Banana Pro** (o Midjourney / FLUX / Imagen 3).

> 💡 **Función de este Plate:** Generar una placa limpia, nítida de borde a borde y sin personas, para que luego las tomas de Mateo (guardia marcial, planos medios y zapateo) puedan componerse sobre este entorno con perfecta continuidad luminosa y atmosférica.

### 🌟 PROMPT MAESTRO (ENGLISH PROMPT - WIDE MASTER PLATE)

```text
A hyper-realistic, perfectly symmetrical wide front-facing camera angle of an empty authentic urban asphalt street in Cochabamba, Bolivia, during the serene cold dawn transition. In the immediate foreground, dark textured asphalt pavement with micro-fissures, fine Andean dust, dry mineral sand particles, concrete sidewalk curbs, and subtle weathered yellow lane paint. In the middleground and background, traditional colonial and rustic neoclassical valley townhouses featuring terracotta clay tile roofs, distressed adobe and textured plaster walls in muted ochre and white tones, dark timber balconies, and silhouetted native Molle and Jacaranda trees along the sidewalks. The distant vanishing point reveals the majestic, hazy silhouette of the Tunari mountain ridge under a deep twilight indigo-blue dawn sky slowly catching a razor-sharp warm amber-gold rim of early morning sunlight on the upper roofs. Cinematic chiaroscuro lighting, subtle atmospheric mountain mist hovering near the cold asphalt. NO PEOPLE. EMPTY STREET. NO CARS. NO VEHICLES. Every element from foreground to background is in perfectly sharp focus (deep depth of field, f/11 aperture, no blur, no bokeh). Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, master prime cinema lens, 8k resolution, raw color grading, ultra-sharp architectural and asphalt textures, photorealistic cinema masterpiece.
```

## 🔍 Variante Especial: Ground Macro Plate (Plano Rasante para Toma 1 - Zapateo)

Para la **Toma 1 (Hook de Shock)** descrita en el guion, donde se requiere un plano macro a ras de suelo para capturar el impacto de la abarca a 120fps y la explosión de arenilla, utiliza este prompt complementario:

```text
An ultra-realistic extreme ground-level macro camera angle resting directly on the textured urban asphalt pavement of a Cochabamba street at dawn. Extreme close-up perspective of the rough, porous asphalt surface, embedded micro-pebbles, loose volcanic dust grains, fine gravel, and concrete curb edge. Low-angle grazing golden amber dawn sunlight skimming across the road texture from the side, creating long dramatic high-contrast micro-shadows and revealing intricate tactile stone textures. Deep cold blue shadows in the background pavement recesses. NO PEOPLE. NO FEET. NO CARS. Completely empty road surface ready for motion plate compositing. Deep depth of field with maximum razor-sharp focus across the entire ground plane (f/11 aperture, no tilt-shift, no blur, no bokeh). Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with 100mm macro cine lens, 8k resolution, tactile photorealistic clarity.
```

## ⚙️ Parámetros Técnicos de Generación

| Parámetro | Configuración Oficial | Justificación |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aspect Ratio:** | `--ar 9:16` | Formato vertical obligatorio para TikTok, Instagram Reels y YouTube Shorts (extraído del `manifest.yaml`). |
| **Apertura de Enfoque:** | `f/11` (Deep Depth of Field) | Todo el encuadre (desde el primer plano hasta las montañas del fondo) debe ser 100% nítido, sin blur ni bokeh artificial. |
| **Negative Prompt:** | `people, humans, dancers, crowds, spectators, vehicles, cars, buses, motorcycles, text, letters, typography, watermark, logo, cartoon, 3d render, anime, blurry, bokeh, shallow depth of field, out of focus, distorted architecture, messy powerlines.` | Evita elementos que rompan la pureza del plate base. |
| **Iluminación:** | *Cold dawn to warm golden rim* | Transición del azul crepuscular andino al primer rayo ámbar del sol de Cochabamba. |
