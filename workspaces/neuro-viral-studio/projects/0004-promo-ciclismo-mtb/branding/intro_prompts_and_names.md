## 10 Prompts de Ingeniería (Video Intro / Logo Animation)
*Estos prompts están diseñados con la técnica de capas: [Sujeto/Acción] + [Entorno/Iluminación] + [Estilo/Cámara] + [Filtros de calidad]. Están en inglés, ya que los modelos de video rinden un 80% mejor en este idioma.*

**Prompt 1: El Cerebro Neón (Fluido y Orgánico)**
> Hyper-realistic cinematic video, exactly 3 seconds. A sleek, abstract glowing neon brain morphs seamlessly into a camera lens. Deep pitch-black background. Subtle volumetric smoke rolls in the background. High-end Hollywood production studio logo intro style. 8k, Unreal Engine 5 render style.

**Prompt 2: La "N" de Fibra Óptica (Agresivo y Tecnológico)**
> Cinematic 3D animation, 3 seconds. An abstract, sharp geometric letter 'N' forms out of nowhere from fast-moving glowing synaptic fiber optics and cyan light trails. Pitch-black background. High contrast, hyper-realistic, cyber-cinematic lighting.

**Prompt 3: El Monolito Metálico (Minimalista y Elegante)**
> Slow cinematic video, 3 seconds. A dark, brushed metallic monolith in the shape of a play button slowly rotates, subtly revealing a hidden letter 'N' in its shadow. Backlit by intense cinematic white spotlights and thick atmospheric fog. Very elegant, sophisticated, and mysterious.

**Prompt 4: La Lente Neuronal (Lujoso y Dorado)**
> Photorealistic video intro, 3 seconds. A futuristic camera lens resembles a glowing golden neuron, radiating elegant energy waves that pulse outwards. Pitch-black background with subtle golden dust particles floating in slow motion in the air. Masterpiece, 8k.

**Prompt 5: Glitch Digital (Para contenido viral/urbano)**
> Fast-paced kinetic video, 2 seconds. A sleek, modern logo glitches into existence through a storm of digital pixels and CRT scanlines. Neon purple and cyan color palette. Heavy chromatic aberration, VHS overlay style, aggressive electronic beat visualizer effect.

**Prompt 6: Tinta en el Agua (Artístico y Profundo)**
> Ultra-slow motion macro video, 4 seconds. Deep black ink drops into crystal clear water, swirling and gracefully taking the shape of a human brain before dissolving into a camera aperture. Ethereal, elegant, cinematic lighting, 8k macro photography.

**Prompt 7: El Iris de la IA (Cibernético y Ocular)**
> Cinematic close-up video, 3 seconds. An extreme macro shot of a cybernetic eye opening. The iris is made of intricate glowing circuits that spin and focus like a camera lens. Cool blue cinematic lighting, dark background, incredibly detailed.

**Prompt 8: Fragmentos de Cristal (Impacto Visual)**
> High-speed phantom camera video, 3 seconds. Thousands of tiny, glowing crystal shards fly in reverse from all edges of the screen, colliding perfectly in the center to form a solid, glowing monolithic block. Cinematic lighting, deep contrast.

**Prompt 9: Horizonte Neón (Retrowave Moderno)**
> Cinematic landscape intro, 3 seconds. A glowing neon grid floor races towards a massive, glowing metallic geometric shape hovering in a dark sky. Subtle fog, synthwave aesthetic but highly polished and modern cinematic quality. 

**Prompt 10: El Eje de Luz (Simple, para agencias de altísima gama)**
> Minimalist cinematic video, 3 seconds. A single, razor-sharp beam of pure white light cuts horizontally across a pitch-black screen, briefly illuminating a sleek, invisible metallic logo in the center before fading to black. Extremely clean, Apple-style premium presentation.
