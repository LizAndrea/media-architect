### Opción 1: El Agujero Negro Supermasivo
* **Logo:** A premium, minimalist flat vector logo for a high-end film production company named "Vortice". A perfect, pitch-black circle at the center, surrounded by a thin, elegant glowing accretion disk of light. Clean geometry, sci-fi aesthetic, sophisticated corporate identity.
* **Imagen:** Epic cinematic wide shot of deep space. A hyper-realistic supermassive black hole bending light and space around it due to extreme gravity. A brilliant, fiery orange and yellow accretion disk of superheated plasma orbits the pitch-black center. Deep space background with subtle nebulae. Volumetric lighting, 8k resolution, IMAX 70mm lens style, photorealistic masterpiece.
* **Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Slow, dramatic push-in. ACTION: The glowing orange plasma of the accretion disk spins violently around the event horizon, pulling cosmic dust and glowing particles into its infinite dark center. Subtle gravitational lensing distorts the background stars.
