# Master Prompts: Branding Audiovisual (Google Flow)

Este documento contiene opciones completas de conceptualización para las 3 marcas finales (**Vibra Studio**, **Vórtice**, y **Viral Z**). 

Cada "Opción" es un ecosistema completo que incluye:
1. **Prompt de Logotipo:** Para generar ideas de diseño vectorial/gráfico del logo.
2. **Prompt de Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Para generar el "fotograma inicial" (16:9) súper realista de la intro del video.
3. **Prompt de Video (VEO - Image-to-Video):** Las instrucciones de movimiento y cámara para darle vida a la imagen anterior.

---

## 🟩 MARCA 1: VIBRA STUDIO
*(Conceptos clave: Frecuencias, sonido, ondas de luz, energía, resonancia).*

### Opción 1: La Onda de Luz (Clásico y Elegante)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Minimalist flat vector logo design for a film studio named "Vibra". The letter 'V' stylized as a smooth, continuous sine wave. Clean lines, neon cyan on a dark background, premium corporate identity. No extra text.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Cinematic 16:9 wide shot. A sleek, glowing neon light-wave floating in the center of a pitch-black, fog-filled room. The wave subtly shapes the letter 'V'. Volumetric lighting, 8k resolution, photorealistic.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Slow push-in. ACTION: The neon wave pulses and vibrates rapidly as if reacting to a deep bass sound, emitting tiny glowing sparks into the dark fog. 

### Opción 2: El Cristal Resonante (Premium y Lujoso)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Luxury cinematic logo for "Vibra Studio". A sharp, faceted quartz crystal where the light refracts to form a 'V'. Monochromatic with subtle rainbow dispersion. High-end, elegant.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Extreme macro shot. A dark, jagged obsidian crystal illuminated from within by a vibrating, warm golden energy. Set against a pure black background. Ultra-detailed, 8k, anamorphic lens.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Slow circular pan. ACTION: The golden energy inside the crystal violently pulses and glows brighter, sending shockwaves of light through the glass facets.

### Opción 3: El Ecualizador Cinematográfico (Moderno)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Modern geometric logo for "Vibra". Vertical equalizer bars of varying heights that create negative space in the shape of a 'V'. Gradient purple to orange. Minimalist.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Cinematic 3D render. Massive glowing vertical LED pillars in a dark, empty warehouse, arranged to form a V shape. Heavy cinematic atmosphere, cinematic smoke, floor reflections.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Low angle, tracking forward. ACTION: The LED pillars light up sequentially in a wave pattern, flashing intensely and illuminating the smoke around them.

### Opción 4: La Cuerda Vibrante (Orgánico)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Abstract minimal logo for "Vibra". A single taut string or wire vibrating, captured in motion blur, forming a subtle 'V'. Black and white, sophisticated.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** High-speed macro photography. A thick, metallic bass string in mid-vibration, glowing with friction heat in a completely dark void. 8k, hyper-detailed.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Static macro. ACTION: The string vibrates intensely, slowing down over 3 seconds until it comes to a complete, sharp stop, releasing a small puff of smoke.

### Opción 5: Pulso de Neón Líquido (Vanguardista)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Liquid typography logo for "Vibra". The 'V' looks like molten, glowing liquid metal. Chrome and neon pink. Y2K modern aesthetic.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Cinematic fluid simulation. A floating sphere of liquid chrome in a dark room, with glowing pink neon veins pulsing beneath its surface. 8k, octane render.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Fast zoom out. ACTION: The liquid chrome sphere violently vibrates and then implodes into the shape of a sleek letter 'V', solidifying instantly.

## 🟪 MARCA 2: VÓRTICE
*(Conceptos clave: Gravedad, agujeros negros, atracción, espirales, portales).*

### Opción 6: El Agujero Negro (Épico e Inmenso)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Minimalist circular logo for "Vortice". A perfect black circle surrounded by a thin, glowing accretion disk of light. Sci-fi, elegant, high-end studio.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Epic cinematic space shot. A glowing, hyper-realistic black hole (vortex) bending light around it. Deep orange and deep space black. Volumetric lighting, 8k, IMAX style.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Slow push-in. ACTION: The vortex spins rapidly, pulling cosmic dust and light into its infinite center. Mesmerizing and smooth rotation.

### Opción 7: El Portal de Agua (Orgánico y Misterioso)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Abstract logo for "Vortice". A continuous spiral line resembling a whirlpool or a fingerprint. Clean vector, dark blue and silver.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Top-down drone shot. A massive, perfect whirlpool forming in the middle of a dark, calm, glassy black ocean. Cinematic moonlight reflecting on the swirling water. 8k.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Static top-down. ACTION: The whirlpool spins faster and deeper, dragging the water into a dark, bottomless center. Water splashes aggressively at the edges.

### Opción 8: El Túnel de Luz (Velocidad)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Geometric logo for "Vortice". A series of concentric triangles creating a deep tunnel optical illusion. Flat vector, neon cyan.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** First-person view inside a massive, dark tunnel made of glowing cyan neon rings. Infinite depth. Cinematic fog, 8k resolution.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Extremely fast forward tracking. ACTION: The camera rushes through the glowing tunnel at light-speed, rings flashing by, creating a hypnotic vortex effect.

### Opción 9: Humo Invertido (Magia visual)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Organic logo for "Vortice". The letter 'V' formed by swirling smoke or clouds. Minimalist, premium, monochromatic.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Cinematic studio shot. Thick, volumetric white smoke swirling in a perfect vortex shape in the center of a pitch-black room. Dramatic single spotlight from above.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Slow pan right. ACTION: The smoke vortex spins aggressively like a tornado, tightening its shape until it forms a solid, recognizable letter 'V', then dissipates.

### Opción 10: La Lente de Gravedad (Audiovisual Puro)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Camera lens logo for "Vortice". The aperture blades of a camera lens spiraling inwards like a vortex. Sleek, titanium and gold.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Extreme close-up of a high-end cinema camera lens. Inside the glass, light is bending and swirling in a vortex pattern. Cinematic lighting, 8k macro.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Macro push-in. ACTION: The aperture blades of the lens rapidly close and open in a spiral motion, while the light inside spins like a miniature galaxy.

## 🟥 MARCA 3: VIRAL Z
*(Conceptos clave: Glitch, velocidad, Generación Z, urbano, cyberpunk, impacto).*

### Opción 11: El Glitch de Neón (Agresivo y Urbano)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Cyberpunk logo for "Viral Z". The letter 'Z' with a heavy digital glitch effect, sliced horizontally. Neon green and magenta on black. Vector art.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Cinematic 16:9. A massive, glowing neon 'Z' in a dark, empty cyberpunk alleyway. Puddles reflecting the neon light. Heavy atmospheric rain. 8k.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Handheld, shaky. ACTION: The neon 'Z' aggressively glitches, flickers, and sparks violently. Digital CRT distortion lines briefly scramble the image.

### Opción 12: Velocidad de la Luz (Rápido y Dinámico)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Speed logo for "Viral Z". The letter 'Z' angled forward with speed lines, looking like a lightning bolt. Minimalist, sharp, aggressive.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Cinematic long-exposure shot. Red and white light trails from fast-moving cars on a dark highway, perfectly forming the shape of a 'Z' in the night. 8k, photorealistic.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Static wide shot. ACTION: The light trails streak past at immense speed, glowing intensely, leaving a burning 'Z' shape suspended in the air.

### Opción 13: El Virus Digital (Contagio Visual)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Tech logo for "Viral Z". A geometric 'Z' made of connecting nodes and circuitry lines that look like they are spreading. Cyan and dark blue.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Macro cinematic shot. A dark metallic surface where glowing, bright blue digital circuitry is spreading like a fast-moving virus, forming a 'Z'. Highly detailed, 8k.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Macro tracking. ACTION: The glowing blue circuitry rapidly grows, branches out, and infects the entire metallic surface, flashing brightly.

### Opción 14: Spray Paint / Urbano (Rebelde)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Streetwear logo for "Viral Z". The letter 'Z' drawn in a raw, aggressive, dripping spray paint style. High contrast black and white.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Cinematic shot. A rough concrete wall in the dark. A freshly painted, glowing fluorescent red 'Z' drips wet paint. Volumetric spotlight illuminating the wall.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Slow push-in. ACTION: The fluorescent paint continues to drip down the wall, glowing intensely in the dark. A puff of spray-paint dust lingers in the air.

### Opción 15: Pantalla Rota (Impacto)
* **Prompt Logotipo:** Edgy logo for "Viral Z". A solid 'Z' that looks like shattered glass. Clean, modern, high impact.
* **Prompt Imagen Base (Nano Banana Pro):** Cinematic close-up. A pitch-black TV screen with a massive, realistic glass shatter in the exact shape of a 'Z'. Bright white light aggressively bleeding through the cracks.
* **Prompt Video (VEO):** CAMERA: Static macro. ACTION: The glass cracks spread further. Suddenly, the screen explodes outward in extreme slow motion, sending glass shards flying toward the lens.
